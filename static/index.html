Privacy & visibility

Discovery is off until you choose to enable it in My story. Your email, surname, birth details, full birth name, location and reflection answers remain private.

Deleting your account removes your profile and sessions from the live database. Backup retention is managed separately by the operator.

Astrology and numerology are for entertainment and reflection. Scores do not predict relationship success. Birth time and location are saved for future chart support; this version calculates an approximate sun sign only.

Privacy & community

Your email, surname, birth details and reflections are private. Discovery is opt-in. Delete your account to remove stored account data.
