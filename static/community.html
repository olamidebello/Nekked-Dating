Safe dating

Keep sensitive information private. Never send money to someone you have not met. Meet in a public place, arrange your own transportation, and tell someone you trust about your plans. Respect boundaries and consent.

Frequently asked questions

When can I view compatibility details?

After an accepted Like or reciprocal interest creates an active match. Unmatching or blocking removes access.

Can I pause my profile?

Yes. Settings → Pause stops new discovery while existing matches and conversations remain available.

How do I report after unmatching?

Use Help and provide the profile reference or relevant details. You can also report from Discover or an active conversation.

Can I install this on my phone?

Use Install app or your browser’s Add to home screen menu where supported.

Trust & safety

Reporting records a moderation case. Blocking stops discovery, matching and conversation access between both accounts. Reports are reviewed by the configured operator; a submitted report is not an automated finding.

How we connect people

Discover offers eligible adult profiles. Send a Like or Rose to a profile, photo or prompt. A recipient can accept or decline. Accepted or reciprocal interests create a match. Symbol comparisons are then calculated automatically. Age, race, language and religion do not affect potential scores.

Identity & respect

Share only the profile information you want to disclose. Respect people’s names, pronouns, identities and boundaries. Optional identity fields do not change compatibility scores.

Privacy & data choices

Birth details, surname, full birth name, private reflections and account email are private. You choose which optional details appear on your discovery profile. Export your account data in Settings. Delete your account from Privacy. Backup retention is controlled separately by the operator.

Community terms

Adults only. Provide truthful information, respect consent, and do not harass, threaten, scam or impersonate others. Symbolic interpretations are for reflection. No subscription is charged without a configured purchase provider and an explicit checkout.

Accessibility

Navigation uses labeled controls and keyboard-accessible actions. Use your device’s zoom, text scaling and screen reader. Send accessibility issues through Help.
