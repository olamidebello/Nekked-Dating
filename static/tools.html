How to read the chart

Expression describes traditional self-expression themes; Soul Urge uses vowels for inner motivations; Personality uses consonants for outward presentation. Hidden Passion identifies the most frequent letter values; Karmic Lessons lists absent values. These names describe symbolic conventions, not evidence of ability or moral debt.

Pinnacles combine month/day, day/year, the first two pinnacles, then month/year. Challenges use absolute differences of single-digit birth components. First stage ends at age 36 minus the single-digit life-path root, followed by two nine-year stages and an open-ended fourth. Age ranges are inclusive.

Detected Karmic Debt totals are 13, 14, 16 and 19 encountered while reducing displayed chart totals. They are labeled observations under these rules, not predictions or obligations.
